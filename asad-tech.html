Security Assistant — Test

Koi bhi photo choose karein — Security Assistant bataye ga ke ye photo AuraGallery ke liye theek hai ya block hogi.

Ye check aap ke phone/browser mein hota hai. Photo kahin upload nahi hoti, koi AI key nahi lagti, koi kharch nahi.

Is test mein photo kahin save nahi hoti. V1 mein: BLOCKED photo upload nahi hogi aur Asad ko khabar mil jaye gi; SAFE photo upload ho jaye gi.

Ye kaise kaam karta hai?

Security Assistant har photo ko check karta hai — theek ho to upload hogi, ghalat ho to photo block ho jati hai. Ye check free aur open-source hai (nsfwjs), aur is mein koi AI key nahi lagti.

Sachai: ye check phone mein hota hai, is liye koi technical banda is ko bypass kar sakta hai. Is liye V1 ke baad server-side check bhi aaye ga. 20 logon ka test group aap ke jan'ne wale log hain, is liye ye test ke liye kaafi hai.

Terms of Use — AuraGallery

AuraGallery by Asad Tech — your photos and videos, safe in the cloud.

1. What you can store

Your own photos and videos. 1GB free for every user. Paid plans: Starter 50GB Rs 299/month, Plus 200GB Rs 799/month, Pro 1TB Rs 2,499/month.

2. What is not allowed

No adult or sexual content, no illegal content, and no photos or videos of other people without their permission. Content that breaks these rules is blocked on upload and the account is closed.

3. Privacy

Only you can see your photos. No human looks at them. An automated safety check runs on every upload to enforce rule 2.

4. Your account

One phone number, one account. You are responsible for keeping your login safe. Asad Tech can update these terms at any time.

5. Plan khatam hone par

Plan lete waqt aap is baat par sign (agree) karte hain: "Maine ye plan liya hai. Agar main agle mahine pay na karun to plan khatam hone ke 30 din baad mera wo data auto-delete ho jaye ga jo 1GB free se zyada hai — misaal: 50GB plan par 49GB delete hoga, 1GB free rahe ga." 30 din mein aap renew ya download kar sakte hain.

About Us

Asad Tech is a technology company from Pakistan. We build software and digital products that solve real problems for real people — shop owners, students and everyday users.

The company was founded by Muhammad Asad, who started Asad Tech with one simple idea: good technology should not be hard, costly or confusing. It should be easy to use and easy to trust.

Today, Asad Tech's products include a digital store, billing software for shops and an AI assistant — and we are working to bring more useful products to everyone.
